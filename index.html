About

Design is my way of making the world feel a little smoother, a little smarter, and a lot more joyful.

Background

Born in India, raised in Singapore for 18 years, and now studying in London, and I bring a diverse, global perspective to everything I do. My fascination with emerging technology and how it shapes everyday experiences led me to pursue BA (Hons) UX Design at UAL.

How I design

Life is already complex enough, so design should solve problems, not add to them. I believe in intuitive, task-driven design that respects shrinking attention spans. The goal: reduce the learning curve and boost user satisfaction.

For me, research and empathy are the heart of it, and accessibility and inclusivity aren't optional, they're foundational.
